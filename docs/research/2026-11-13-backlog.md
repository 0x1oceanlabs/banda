# Backlog: work to plan after the three tracks

Kept 2026-11-13, at Roma's ask. [The three tracks](2026-11-13-three-tracks.md) cover about ten weeks. This file holds what they leave out, so nothing found on the way is lost. It is not a plan. Nothing here has an order, a size that was checked or a date.

**How it is used.**

- An item is planned when its trigger comes: a slice runs into it, a story needs it, or Roma picks it. Planning it means a slice in the roadmap or a plan of its own, and then it leaves this file.
- After week 10, Roma goes through this file with a session and chooses what comes next.
- A session that finds work no slice needs adds it here, in one line with its source, rather than starting it.
- Each item names the principle or story of [what we build](../what-we-build.md) it serves.

## 1. The engine from the inside

Five findings of the engine map (2026-11-12, kept outside the repository in `~/Banda/artifacts/engine-map/2-mess.md`) that no slice of the three tracks needs. None shows outside, so none is urgent; each makes the engine harder to read or change. Candidates for a fourth track, "the engine from the inside", to decide after week 10. The first that a slice runs into goes first; SE11 (engines carry the move) may well run into the turn.

| Finding | What is wrong | Harm | Size | Principle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Settings: two doors, no lock** (map 6) | This device's keys go page → main → a `banda settings set` child → file → a `banda look --follow` child; a machine's keys go through the engine as a `call` with no receipt and no fact. Both, and the accounts store, end in `banda_settings::set` in three processes with no lock | lost updates between processes (#334); six hops for one save; a page without Electron cannot write this device's settings | M | 4 |
| **Five ways to hear a change; Projects outside the log** (map 8) | durable listeners, the raw `committed()` broadcast, window `subscribe`, untyped streams, memory-only `doing`; `projects.*` keep no receipt and emit no fact, so clients poll; "machine" is a fake Project | how a change reaches the window depends on which of five ways applies; a Project added on arc reaches the Mac on the next poll; a retried create can make two | M | 2, 4 |
| **A turn is an hour-long outbox job** (map 9) | `turn.start` holds its outbox key for the whole turn; the Agent's own turn is a bare spawn with a second restart path; the queue is spread over outbox rows, turn state and a flag; Questions are rows plus oneshots in memory; `effects::work` does eight jobs; two ACP stacks and two Harness pools | a crash or restart cuts running turns and holds their queues; every "stop came while it started" bug is a special case; the hardest path in the product to read | L | 4, 7 |
| **The log carries streams; a usage mirror in the machine store** (map 10) | tool output is rewritten into the log every 80 ms (7 MB of one Project's log); `harnesses.usage_changed` with a null payload on every journal read (most of arc's machine events); a 92-day usage mirror makes the machine store about 20 times its log | replays carry megabytes; the one writer is busy while a long run is watched | M (the pings and the mirror S each) | 4, 9 |
| **One command is three jobs on the Project's one writer** (map 12) | the receipt check, the write and the answer are three writer jobs, and the fact can reach a client before the answer | while turns stream, every click queues three times | M | 4 |
