# No Space Threads follow the sidebar preset

Roma, 2026-11-04: Threads are already grouped by the sidebar preset; a separate No Space section looks wrong, especially in Inbox.

This supersedes ADR 0046 decision 2's unconditional No Space group. With grouping by state or no grouping, scratch Threads join the same sections, sorting and shelves as ordinary Threads. A Space filter applies to them too. Only grouping by Space collects them under No Space, instead of displaying their unnamed scratch Spaces separately.

Scratch Spaces stay out of Space pickers and the list of named Spaces. The New Thread picker still offers No Space. Promotion and on-disk folders do not change.
